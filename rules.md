# Edit rules

Every note about the edit that should apply to future videos goes here, one line, with the date.
Claude reads this file before every edit. Copy it to your project and start adding your own.

Examples of rules that came out of real feedback:

- 2026-10-27: captions lower, they were covering the chin. Never on the mouth.
- 2026-10-27: the highlight behind the active word flickered on short words. It now skips words under 0.15 s.
- 2026-10-27: no jokes on screen that I did not say. Only my speech and the hook.
- 2026-10-27: sentences I recorded on purpose stay. Flag a risk once, I decide.
- 2026-10-27: pick the take by the picture too, not only by the transcript. The best take is the one where I look at the lens.
- 2026-11-01: show me the edit plan before rendering. Do not choose effects silently.
